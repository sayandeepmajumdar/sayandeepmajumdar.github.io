UTM Tracking Best Practices & Guidelines

1. Consistency & Lowercase

Analytics tools treat Facebook, facebook, and FB as separate traffic sources. Always standardize on lowercase naming across your entire marketing team.

2. Don't Track Internal Links

Never put UTM parameters on internal website links (e.g. banners linking to your own pricing page). Doing so resets the user's original referrer session and skews conversion attribution.

3. Standard Mediums

Use industry-standard mediums recognized by GA4 Default Channel Groups: cpc (paid search), paid_social (ad campaigns), social (organic posts), and email.
