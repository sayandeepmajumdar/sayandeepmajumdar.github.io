📐 How is EMI Calculated?

EMI is computed using the standard reducing-balance formula: EMI = P × r × (1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n - 1), where interest is charged only on the outstanding principal balance each month.

⏳ Why Are Early EMIs Interest-Heavy?

Since the outstanding principal balance is highest at the beginning of the loan, the interest portion dominates initial monthly payments. Over time, principal payments accelerate.

💡 The Power of Prepayment

Even small additional monthly payments or occasional lump-sum prepayments directly reduce the principal balance, saving significant interest and shaving years off your loan tenure.
