LCM Calculator

Find the least common multiple (LCM) for multiple integers instantly.

🔢 LCM Formula

LCM(a, b) = |a × b| / GCF(a, b)

How to Find the LCM

The LCM of two integers is the smallest positive integer that is divisible by both numbers.
