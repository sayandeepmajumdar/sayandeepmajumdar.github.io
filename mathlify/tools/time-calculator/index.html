Time Calculator

Add or subtract time durations, calculate date &amp; time projections, and find the exact duration between two dates.

⏱️ 3 Powerful Modes

1. Add/Subtract Time: Combine or deduct durations in days, hours, minutes, and seconds.
2. Time From Date: Project future or past timestamps by adding or subtracting elapsed time.
3. Between Dates: Measure the exact span separating two calendar dates.

📋 Conversion Factors
  • 1 Day = 24 Hours = 1,440 Minutes
  • 1 Hour = 60 Minutes = 3,600 Seconds
  • 1 Minute = 60 Seconds
  • 1 Day = 86,400 Seconds
  • 1 Week = 7 Days = 168 Hours

How Time Calculation Works

Unlike base-10 decimal mathematics, time measurement uses a mixed-radix sexagesimal system (base-60 for minutes and seconds, base-24 for hours in a day, and 7 days in a standard week). Adding or subtracting time durations requires carrying over or borrowing across these distinct unit thresholds.

Duration Carryover & Borrowing

When adding, every 60 seconds rolls into 1 minute, every 60 minutes rolls into 1 hour, and every 24 hours rolls into 1 day. When subtracting, if a lower unit is smaller than the subtracted amount, 1 unit is borrowed from the next higher unit (e.g. 1 minute becomes 60 seconds).

Exact Date & Calendar Math

When calculating across calendar boundaries, our tool automatically accounts for month day-counts (28, 29, 30, or 31 days) and Gregorian leap years, providing precise resulting timestamps, weekdays, and elapsed intervals.

Common Applications

  • Payroll & Work Timesheets: Summing logged shifts, project hours, and overtime across multiple tasks.
  • Travel & Flight Schedules: Calculating flight layovers, arrival times across time zones, and trip durations.
  • Audio & Video Production: Totaling media runtimes, podcast episode segments, and cut timestamps.
  • Athletic Training: Computing marathon splits, lap totals, and pace increments.
