Surface Area Calculator

Calculate total, lateral, and base surface area of 3D geometric solids including spheres, cylinders, cones, rectangular boxes, cubes, and capsules.

📦 Surface Area Formulas
Sphere: SA = 4πr²
Cylinder: SA = 2πr² + 2πrh
Cone: SA = πr² + πr√(r² + h²)
Box: SA = 2(lw + lh + wh)
🌐 Lateral vs Base Area
Base Area: The area of bottom and top faces.
Lateral Area: The area of all sides excluding the bases (e.g. the curved jacket of a cylinder).

Surface Area in Three Dimensions

Surface area is the total area that the surface of an object occupies in three-dimensional space. It represents the total amount of 2D material required to wrap or paint the external boundary of a solid.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the surface area of a sphere?

Archimedes proved that the surface area of a sphere of radius r is exactly equal to four times the area of its great circle: SA = 4πr².
