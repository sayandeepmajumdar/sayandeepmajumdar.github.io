Rules & Trust Model

Standard Tic-Tac-Toe

  • Players take turns placing their mark (✕ or ◯) in an empty 3x3 cell.
  • First player to align 3 of their marks horizontally, vertically, or diagonally wins the round.
  • If all 9 cells are filled with no 3-in-a-row, the round ends in a Draw.

🛡️ Decentralized Trust Model

Because moves are turn-based and public, both browsers validate each move independently:

  • Verifies it is genuinely the sender's active turn.
  • Verifies target cell index is valid (0–8) and currently empty.
  • Guarantees neither client can overwrite filled squares or double-move.
