How Fair Play Works

Our built-in system guarantees 100% honest gameplay

1. Why Anti-Cheat Matters: In regular online number guessing, an opponent could secretly change their number mid-game to make you lose. ArcadeLab makes cheating impossible!
2. Secret Number Locking: Before any guessing starts, the host's browser generates a secure digital lock for the chosen number. Once locked, the number cannot be altered.
3. Automatic Round Verification: When the round ends, the secret number is revealed, and the game automatically verifies that all Higher / Lower hints were completely honest and accurate.
4. Hot / Cold Temperature Radar: Watch the temperature gauge after each guess to see how close you are to the secret target:
🔥 Burning Hot: Super close (within 2%) 🌡️ Hot: Very close (within 5%) 🌤️ Warm: Getting closer (within 15%) ❄️ Cold / Ice: Far away
