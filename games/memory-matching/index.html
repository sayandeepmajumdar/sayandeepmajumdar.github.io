How to Play

Rules of Online Memory Match

1. The Goal: Find the most matching card pairs by flipping 2 cards on your turn.
2. Turn Flow & Combos: • If your 2 flipped cards MATCH: You score a point, build a combo streak, and take another turn!
• If they DON'T MATCH: Both cards are shown for 1 second, then flip face-down and your turn ends.
3. Winning the Match: The player with the most pairs found when all cards are cleared wins the duel and earns ELO ranking points!
