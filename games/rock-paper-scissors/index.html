Rules & Anti-Cheat Protocol

Classic RPS Rules

  • Rock (🪨) beats Scissors (crushes)
  • Scissors (✂️) beats Paper (cuts)
  • Paper (📄) beats Rock (covers)

Extended RPSLS Rules

  • Scissors cuts Paper & decapitates Lizard
  • Paper covers Rock & disproves Spock
  • Rock crushes Scissors & crushes Lizard
  • Lizard poisons Spock & eats Paper
  • Spock smashes Scissors & vaporizes Rock

🛡️ Cryptographic Anti-Cheat

Your client generates SHA256(choice + salt + roundId) and exchanges only the hash first. Once both players lock in, moves & salts are revealed and cryptographically verified.
