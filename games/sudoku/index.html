How to Play

Rules & Sudoku Duel Modes

Sudoku Rule: Fill the 9x9 grid so every row, column, and 3x3 box contains digits 1 to 9 without repeats. (In 6x6 Mini, fill digits 1 to 6 in 2x3 boxes).
Mode 1: Race to Solve (Head-to-Head) Both players get the exact same puzzle and solve independently. The live race bar displays your opponent's progress without spoiling cell answers. First to complete 100% wins!
Mode 2: Co-op Live Board (Collaborative) Both players solve the same grid together in real time. Placed numbers and cursor highlights sync across screens instantly.
Pencil Notes & Keyboard Shortcuts: Toggle ✏️ Notes mode or press N to jot down candidate digits. Use Arrow keys to navigate and 1-9 to input.
