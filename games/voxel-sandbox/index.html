🧱 Chunk Meshing & Diff Sync

  • Face-Culled Chunk Geometry: Only visible exposed block faces are generated, keeping draw calls minimal and rendering buttery at 60FPS.
  • P2P Single-Block Diffs: World generation is 100% deterministic from the shared seed; block placements are broadcast as 4-byte diffs `{x,y,z,type}`.
