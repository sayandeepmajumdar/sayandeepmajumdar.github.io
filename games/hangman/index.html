How Fair Play Works

Our built-in system guarantees 100% honest gameplay

1. Why Anti-Cheat Matters: In regular online hangman, an opponent could secretly change their word mid-game to make you lose. ArcadeLab makes cheating mathematically impossible!
2. Secret Word Locking: Before guessing starts, the Setter's browser generates a secure digital lock for the chosen word. Once locked, the word cannot be modified.
3. Automated Letter Verification: Letter hits and positions are computed automatically by the browser software, removing any chance of human misreporting.
4. Role Alternation: Players automatically switch roles (Setter vs Guesser) each round for balanced, competitive matches!
