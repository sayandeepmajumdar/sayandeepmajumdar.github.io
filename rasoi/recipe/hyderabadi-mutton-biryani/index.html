Hyderabadi Mutton Dum Biryani

5.0 (1,920 ratings)
• Cuisine: Hyderabadi

Tender young goat meat marinated with raw papaya tenderizer, yogurt, fried onions, and Nizami spices, layered with saffron basmati rice and slow-cooked in a sealed dum pot.

⏱️ Prep Time 45 min
🍳 Cook Time 60 min
⌛ Total Time 105 min
🍽️ Base Servings 6
👨‍🍳 Difficulty Hard
🌶️ Spice Level Spicy
🥕

Ingredients

Servings:
6

For Mutton Marination (Kacchi Gosht)

  • 800 g tender goat meat (mutton, mix of ribs and shank)— bone-in, cut into chunks
  • 2 tbsp raw papaya paste (with skin)— natural enzyme tenderizer for mutton
  • 250 g whisked thick curd— full fat
  • 3 tbsp ginger-garlic paste— freshly pounded
  • 150 g fried onions (birista)— crushed
  • 1 1/2 cups fresh mint & coriander— chopped
  • 1 tsp shahi jeera— whole
  • 1 batch cardamom, cloves, cinnamon, mace— freshly ground
  • 2 tbsp Kashmiri red chilli powder— deep color
  • 4 tbsp ghee & onion oil— richness
  • 2 tsp salt— to taste

For Basmati Rice & Aromatics

  • 500 g aged long-grain Basmati rice— soaked 1 hour
  • 25 threads saffron strands— steeped in warm milk
  • 3 tbsp pure ghee— for top layer
🔥

Cooking Instructions

  1. 01

    Marinate Mutton with Raw Papaya

    ⏱️ 240m

    Toss bone-in mutton with raw papaya paste, ginger-garlic paste, yogurt, crushed fried onions, ground spices, mint, coriander, ghee, and salt. Refrigerate for at least 4 hours (ideally overnight). The papain enzymes tenderize the meat fibers.

    ✓ Visual Cue: Mutton softens and absorbs deep spiced aroma
  2. 02

    Parboil Rice to 70%

    ⏱️ 6m

    Cook soaked basmati rice in salted boiling water with whole spices for 5-6 minutes until 70% cooked. Drain well.

    ✓ Visual Cue: Rice has elongated fully with a firm bite
  3. 03

    Layer Over Mutton in Handi

    ⏱️ 5m

    Layer the hot rice over the marinated mutton. Drizzle saffron milk, melted ghee, fried onions, and fresh mint leaves.

    ✓ Visual Cue: Glistening royal layers ready for slow steaming
  4. 04

    Slow Dum on Tawa

    ⏱️ 55m

    Seal handi tightly with dough and lid. Cook on high heat for 10 minutes, then transfer over a heavy iron tawa on low heat for 45-50 minutes to cook the mutton through.

    ✓ Visual Cue: The lid swells with intense pressure and heavenly aroma escapes the seal
  5. 05

    Rest & Serve

    ⏱️ 15m

    Rest for 15 minutes before opening. Gently uncover and scoop mutton pieces and saffron rice onto platters.

    ✓ Visual Cue: Mutton falls off the bone with melting tenderness
💡

Chef's Authentic Tips

  • Raw papaya paste is indispensable for Kacchi Mutton Biryani; without it, mutton will remain tough during dum cooking.
  • Use a mix of chops, ribs, and marrow bones for ultimate richness.
🔄

Smart Substitutions

  • Can use lamb if goat meat is unavailable, reducing marination by 1 hour.
🍽️

Serving Suggestions & Pairings

  • ✓ Serve hot with Mirchi Ka Salan, boiled eggs, and burani raita (garlic yogurt).
