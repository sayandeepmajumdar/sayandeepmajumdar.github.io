Shahi Paneer

4.7 (790 ratings)
• Cuisine: Mughlai

Royal Mughlai preparation of cottage cheese in a luxurious, creamy, aromatic gravy made with cashew nuts, melon seeds, mild spices, and scented with saffron.

⏱️ Prep Time 15 min
🍳 Cook Time 25 min
⌛ Total Time 40 min
🍽️ Base Servings 4
👨‍🍳 Difficulty Medium
🌶️ Spice Level Mild
🥕

Ingredients

Servings:
4

For the Paneer

  • 350 g fresh paneer— cut into triangles or thick cubes

For the Royal White-Golden Paste

  • 2 medium onions— boiled in water with 1 cardamom for 8 mins, then drained and pureed
  • 20 g cashews— soaked in warm water
  • 1 tbsp magaz (melon seeds)— soaked with cashews
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste— smooth

For the Shahi Gravy

  • 2 tbsp pure ghee— for royal fragrance
  • 3 whole green cardamom— crushed
  • 1/2 tsp shahi jeera (caraway seeds)— fine royal cumin
  • 150 ml tomato puree— strained for mild tang and color
  • 1 tsp coriander powder— ground
  • 0.8 tsp Kashmiri red chilli powder— for mild warmth
  • 15 threads saffron strands— steeped in 2 tbsp warm milk
  • 3 tbsp whisked curd (yogurt)— room temperature
  • 60 ml fresh cream— heavy cream
  • 1 tsp kasuri methi— crushed
  • 0.8 tsp sugar or honey— delicate sweetness
  • 1 tsp salt— to taste
🔥

Cooking Instructions

  1. 01

    Boil Aromatics & Puree

    ⏱️ 10m

    Boil roughly diced onions in 1 cup water with a pinch of salt and 1 cardamom pod for 8 minutes until translucent. Drain well, cool, and blend along with soaked cashews and melon seeds into a velvety smooth pale paste.

    ✓ Visual Cue: Boiling removes raw pungent sulfur, producing a mild naturally sweet base
  2. 02

    Cook the Paste in Ghee

    ⏱️ 8m

    Heat ghee in a pan over medium heat. Crackle shahi jeera and crushed cardamoms. Add ginger-garlic paste and sauté for 1 minute. Add the onion-cashew paste and cook on medium-low heat for 6-8 minutes, stirring continuously so cashews do not stick.

    ✓ Visual Cue: The paste becomes glossy and leaves the pan sides
  3. 03

    Add Spices, Tomato & Curd

    ⏱️ 5m

    Lower flame, stir in tomato puree, coriander powder, Kashmiri chilli, and whisked yogurt. Stir vigorously to prevent yogurt curdling. Cook for 5 minutes until gravy turns pale orange and aromatic.

    ✓ Visual Cue: Tiny beads of golden ghee appear along the surface
  4. 04

    Add Saffron & Paneer

    ⏱️ 4m

    Pour in saffron infused milk and 1/2 cup warm water to achieve velvety gravy consistency. Slide in paneer pieces, salt, and sugar. Simmer covered on low heat for 4 minutes so paneer absorbs the regal sauce.

    ✓ Visual Cue: Gravy takes on a lovely delicate golden glow
  5. 05

    Enrich with Cream & Kasuri Methi

    ⏱️ 2m

    Stir in crushed kasuri methi and fresh cream. Give one gentle stir, turn off heat, and let rest 2 minutes before serving.

    ✓ Visual Cue: Unbelievably silky texture scented with saffron and cardamom
💡

Chef's Authentic Tips

  • Boiling onions rather than frying them raw is essential for authentic Shahi gravy—it keeps the flavor mild, creamy, and sweet.
  • Use shahi jeera (caraway seeds) rather than regular cumin for an authentic Mughlai aroma.
🔄

Smart Substitutions

  • If melon seeds (magaz) are unavailable, increase cashews by 10g or use blanched peeled almonds.
🍽️

Serving Suggestions & Pairings

  • ✓ Garnish with slivered toasted almonds and saffron strands.
  • ✓ Serve with sheermal, butter naan, or saffron pulao.
