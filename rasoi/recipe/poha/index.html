Maharashtrian Kanda Poha

4.8 (1,140 ratings)
• Cuisine: Maharashtrian

Light, fluffy flattened rice seasoned with mustard seeds, turmeric, crunchy roasted peanuts, green chillies, curry leaves, and sweet softened onions.

⏱️ Prep Time 10 min
🍳 Cook Time 10 min
⌛ Total Time 20 min
🍽️ Base Servings 4
👨‍🍳 Difficulty Easy
🌶️ Spice Level Medium
🥕

Ingredients

Servings:
4

For Rinsing Poha

  • 250 g thick flattened rice (jada poha)— thick variety, rinsed gently in colander and drained
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric powder— tossed with rinsed poha for even sunny color
  • 1 tsp sugar— tossed with poha
  • 1 tsp salt— to taste

For the Tempering & Veggies

  • 2 tbsp peanut oil or cooking oil— peanut oil adds authentic aroma
  • 3 tbsp raw peanuts— fried crisp
  • 1 tsp mustard seeds— black
  • 1/2 tsp cumin seeds— whole
  • 0.3 tsp asafoetida (hing)— pinch
  • 12 leaves curry leaves— fresh
  • 3 whole green chillies— finely chopped
  • 2 medium onions (kanda)— finely chopped
  • 1 medium potatoes (batata)— peeled and finely diced
  • 1 1/2 tbsp fresh lemon juice— fresh
  • 3 tbsp fresh grated coconut & coriander— for topping
🔥

Cooking Instructions

  1. 01

    Rinse & Season Poha

    ⏱️ 4m

    Place thick poha in a colander and rinse gently under running water for 30 seconds. Drain completely. Sprinkle turmeric, sugar, and salt over the damp poha; toss gently with fingers so every flake is coated.

    ✓ Visual Cue: Poha softens into fluffy individual flakes without turning into paste
  2. 02

    Fry Peanuts Crisp

    ⏱️ 3m

    Heat 2 tbsp oil in a wide kadai. Add raw peanuts and fry on low flame until crunchy and golden brown. Drain half the peanuts for topping.

    ✓ Visual Cue: Peanuts crackle and turn aromatic amber
  3. 03

    Sauté Aromatics & Potatoes

    ⏱️ 7m

    In the same oil, crackle mustard seeds, cumin, hing, and curry leaves. Add diced potatoes. Cover and cook for 4-5 minutes until potatoes are tender. Add chopped onions and green chillies; cook for 3 minutes until onions turn soft and translucent.

    ✓ Visual Cue: Sweet softened onions and crisp-tender potato cubes
  4. 04

    Steam-Heat Poha

    ⏱️ 3m

    Tip the seasoned, fluffy poha into the pan. Toss gently from the bottom with a flat spatula to distribute onions and spices. Sprinkle 1 tbsp water, cover tightly with a lid, and steam on lowest flame for 2-3 minutes.

    ✓ Visual Cue: Poha warms through thoroughly, becoming light and cloud-soft
  5. 05

    Garnish & Serve

    ⏱️ 1m

    Remove lid, drizzle fresh lemon juice, reserved crunchy peanuts, fresh grated coconut, and chopped coriander leaves. Serve hot.

    ✓ Visual Cue: Vibrant sunny yellow breakfast crowned with green herbs and white coconut
💡

Chef's Authentic Tips

  • Use thick poha (jada poha), never thin poha; thin poha disintegrates into mush when rinsed.
  • Tossing salt, sugar, and turmeric with the damp poha before cooking ensures uniform color without vigorous stirring.
🔄

Smart Substitutions

  • Omit potatoes for classic Kanda Poha, or add boiled green peas for Matar Poha.
🍽️

Serving Suggestions & Pairings

  • ✓ Serve hot with a wedge of lemon, crisp sev, and hot masala chai.
