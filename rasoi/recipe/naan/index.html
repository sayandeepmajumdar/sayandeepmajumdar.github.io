Garlic Butter Naan

4.9 (1,390 ratings)
• Cuisine: North Indian / Tandoori

Soft, pillowy, blistered flatbread cooked on high heat, brushed with melted butter, minced garlic, and fresh coriander.

⏱️ Prep Time 20 min
🍳 Cook Time 15 min
⌛ Total Time 35 min
🍽️ Base Servings 4
👨‍🍳 Difficulty Easy
🌶️ Spice Level Mild
🥕

Ingredients

Servings:
4

For the Naan Dough

  • 300 g all-purpose flour (maida)— sifted
  • 0.8 tsp baking powder— leavening
  • 0.3 tsp baking soda— tenderizer
  • 1 tsp sugar— feeds yeast / assists browning
  • 3 tbsp yogurt (curd)— gives soft crumb
  • 150 ml milk or lukewarm water— for kneading
  • 1 1/2 tbsp oil— vegetable
  • 0.8 tsp salt— fine

For the Garlic Butter Topping

  • 6 cloves garlic— very finely minced
  • 40 g unsalted butter— melted
  • 2 tbsp fresh coriander— finely chopped
  • 1/2 tsp nigella seeds (kalonji)— optional aromatic sprinkle
🔥

Cooking Instructions

  1. 01

    Knead Soft Elastic Dough

    ⏱️ 10m

    Whisk flour, baking powder, baking soda, sugar, and salt in a bowl. Add yogurt and oil. Gradually mix in lukewarm milk and knead for 6-8 minutes until soft, pliable, and slightly tacky. Grease with 1 tsp oil, cover, and rest in a warm spot for 1-2 hours.

    ✓ Visual Cue: Dough springs back gently when pressed with a finger
  2. 02

    Roll & Top with Garlic

    ⏱️ 6m

    Divide dough into 6 equal balls. Roll one ball into a teardrop shape on a lightly floured surface. Sprinkle minced garlic, chopped coriander, and kalonji on top. Roll gently once more with the rolling pin to embed garlic into the dough.

    ✓ Visual Cue: Teardrop flatbread speckled with fresh green coriander and garlic
  3. 03

    Water the Underside

    ⏱️ 1m

    Flip the rolled naan over. Generously brush the backside with water using your fingers or a pastry brush (this allows the naan to stick to the cast-iron tawa, replicating a clay tandoor wall).

    ✓ Visual Cue: Wet surface looks glistening
  4. 04

    Tawa Cook & Inverted Flame Blistering

    ⏱️ 3m

    Heat a heavy iron tawa (not non-stick) on high flame until very hot. Slap wet side onto tawa. In 30-40 seconds, large bubbles will puff up. Invert tawa directly upside down over the open flame, swirling it 2 inches above the fire to roast the top until charred and blistered.

    ✓ Visual Cue: Spectacular smoky charred bubbles puff up all over the surface
  5. 05

    Butter & Serve

    ⏱️ 1m

    Scrape naan off tawa with a flat spatula. Immediately brush generously with melted butter and serve hot.

    ✓ Visual Cue: Buttery sheen soaking into crispy, tender blisters
💡

Chef's Authentic Tips

  • You must use an iron tawa (never non-stick); the dough will slip right off non-stick when you invert it over the flame!
  • Kneading with warm milk and yogurt yields soft bread that stays tender even after cooling.
🔄

Smart Substitutions

  • Omit garlic for classic butter naan, or top with grated cheddar cheese for cheese naan.
🍽️

Serving Suggestions & Pairings

  • ✓ Serve steaming hot with Butter Chicken, Dal Makhani, or Paneer Tikka Masala.
