Mumbai Vada Pav

4.9 (2,150 ratings)
• Cuisine: Maharashtrian / Street Food

Mumbai's beloved street burger: golden, crispy, spiced potato batata vada tucked inside a soft pav, layered with fiery dry garlic chutney and zesty green coriander chutney.

⏱️ Prep Time 20 min
🍳 Cook Time 20 min
⌛ Total Time 40 min
🍽️ Base Servings 4
👨‍🍳 Difficulty Easy
🌶️ Spice Level Spicy
🥕

Ingredients

Servings:
4

For the Batata Vada Filling

  • 400 g potatoes— boiled, peeled, and crumbled coarsely
  • 1 tsp mustard seeds— black
  • 0.3 tsp asafoetida (hing)— pinch
  • 12 leaves curry leaves— finely chopped
  • 3 whole green chillies— crushed with garlic and ginger into thecha paste
  • 6 cloves garlic— crushed
  • 1 inch ginger— crushed
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric powder— bright yellow
  • 3 tbsp fresh coriander— finely chopped
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice— fresh
  • 1 tsp salt— to taste

For the Crispy Besan Batter

  • 150 g gram flour (besan)— sifted
  • 1 1/2 tbsp rice flour— for extra crispness
  • 0.3 tsp turmeric powder & chilli powder— each
  • 1 pinch baking soda— for light puff
  • 1 tbsp hot oil (mohun)— whisked into batter
  • 120 ml water— for smooth coating consistency

For Assembly & Fiery Chutneys

  • 6 buns fresh ladi pav— slit in half
  • 4 tbsp dry garlic coconut chutney (lasun chutney)— the signature red chutney
  • 4 tbsp spicy green chutney & sweet tamarind chutney— each
  • 6 whole salted fried green chillies— for authentic garnish
  • 500 ml oil— for deep frying
🔥

Cooking Instructions

  1. 01

    Cook the Aromatic Potato Masala

    ⏱️ 7m

    Heat 1 tbsp oil. Splutter mustard seeds, hing, and curry leaves. Add crushed ginger-garlic-chilli paste (thecha). Sauté for 1 minute until fragrant. Add turmeric and salt, then fold in crumbled potatoes, lemon juice, and coriander. Mix well, cook 2 minutes, and cool.

    ✓ Visual Cue: Bright yellow, fragrant potato mixture with distinct mustard specks
  2. 02

    Shape Potato Balls

    ⏱️ 3m

    Divide cooled potato mixture into 6 equal round balls (about the size of a golf ball).

    ✓ Visual Cue: Smooth, round balls ready for batter dipping
  3. 03

    Whisk Silky Besan Batter

    ⏱️ 4m

    Whisk besan, rice flour, turmeric, chilli, pinch of baking soda, salt, 1 tbsp hot oil, and water into a smooth batter that coats the back of a spoon cleanly.

    ✓ Visual Cue: Lump-free, smooth ribbon-consistency batter
  4. 04

    Dip & Deep Fry Batata Vada

    ⏱️ 6m

    Heat oil in a kadai over medium flame. Dip each potato ball into batter to coat completely. Drop gently into hot oil. Fry in batches for 4-5 minutes, turning occasionally until golden and crispy.

    ✓ Visual Cue: Evenly golden, puffed, crisp batter shell
  5. 05

    Assemble the Iconic Vada Pav

    ⏱️ 2m

    Slit a pav bun. Smear green chutney on one side, tamarind chutney on the other, and dust generously with fiery dry red garlic chutney. Place a hot batata vada inside and press down gently. Serve with a salted fried green chilli.

    ✓ Visual Cue: Pungent garlic chutney, pillowy soft pav, and hot crunchy vada
💡

Chef's Authentic Tips

  • Pounding fresh garlic, ginger, and green chillies into a coarse paste (thecha) is the real flavor secret of Mumbai vendors.
  • Adding 1 tbsp of hot smoking oil to the besan batter creates a crispier coating that absorbs less oil.
🔄

Smart Substitutions

  • Can use dinner rolls or slider buns if ladi pav is unavailable.
🍽️

Serving Suggestions & Pairings

  • ✓ Serve hot with a steaming cup of cutting masala chai.
