Hyderabadi Chicken Dum Biryani

5.0 (2,850 ratings)
• Cuisine: Hyderabadi / Mughlai

The crown jewel of Nizami cuisine: fragrant aged basmati rice and raw marinated bone-in chicken slow-cooked together in a sealed 'dum' vessel with saffron, mint, and caramelized onions.

⏱️ Prep Time 40 min
🍳 Cook Time 45 min
⌛ Total Time 85 min
🍽️ Base Servings 6
👨‍🍳 Difficulty Hard
🌶️ Spice Level Spicy
🥕

Ingredients

Servings:
6

For the Raw Chicken Marinade (Kacchi Marination)

  • 800 g bone-in chicken (biryani cut pieces)— washed and deeply slashed
  • 200 g thick whisked yogurt (curd)— room temperature
  • 2 1/2 tbsp ginger-garlic paste— freshly ground
  • 150 g birista (crispy fried brown onions)— divided, hand-crumbled into marinade
  • 1 cup fresh mint leaves (pudina)— torn
  • 1 cup fresh coriander leaves— chopped
  • 5 whole green chillies— slit lengthwise
  • 1 tsp shahi jeera (caraway seeds)— whole
  • 6 whole green cardamom— crushed
  • 2 inch cinnamon stick— whole
  • 5 whole cloves— whole
  • 1/2 tsp mace (javitri) & nutmeg— freshly powdered royal spice
  • 1 1/2 tbsp Kashmiri red chilli powder— vibrant heat
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric powder— golden
  • 1 tsp garam masala— aromatic
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice— fresh
  • 3 tbsp oil from frying onions— infused with rich caramelized onion essence
  • 1 1/2 tsp salt— generous

For Parboiling the Basmati Rice

  • 500 g aged long-grain Basmati rice— washed and soaked 45 mins
  • 3 liters water— plenty of rolling boiling water
  • 1 batch whole spices (bay leaf, star anise, cardamom)— tied in muslin or dropped whole
  • 2 tbsp salt— water must taste like seawater

For the Dum Layering & Aromatics

  • 3 tbsp pure desi ghee— melted
  • 20 threads saffron strands— soaked in 1/4 cup warm milk
  • 1 tsp kewra water or rose water— subtle royal essence
🔥

Cooking Instructions

  1. 01

    Marinate Chicken (Overnight or 2 Hours)

    ⏱️ 120m

    In a heavy-bottomed biryani handi, mix chicken with yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, 2/3 of crushed fried onions (birista), half of chopped mint and coriander, slit green chillies, shahi jeera, whole and ground spices, lemon juice, onion-frying oil, and salt. Marinate for at least 2 hours.

    ✓ Visual Cue: Meat is thoroughly coated in thick aromatic spiced yogurt
  2. 02

    Cook Rice to 70% Parboiled

    ⏱️ 6m

    Bring 3 liters of water to a rolling boil with whole spices and 2 tbsp salt. Add drained soaked basmati rice. Cook on high heat for exactly 5-6 minutes until 70% cooked (rice grain has elongated, but has a firm bite and breaks into 3 pieces when pressed).

    ✓ Visual Cue: Long elegant grains with firm center core
  3. 03

    Layer Rice Over Raw Chicken

    ⏱️ 5m

    Using a slotted skimmer, scoop the 70% parboiled hot rice directly over the raw marinated chicken layer in the handi. Spread evenly. Scatter remaining birista, mint, coriander, saffron milk, kewra water, and 3 tbsp desi ghee across the top.

    ✓ Visual Cue: Layer of snow-white and saffron-streaked rice crowned with herbs
  4. 04

    Seal Handi with Dough (Dum Pukht)

    ⏱️ 38m

    Roll a snake of whole wheat dough around the rim of the handi. Press the lid tightly to form an airtight seal. Place handi on high heat for 8 minutes until steam builds up inside, then transfer handi onto a preheated heavy cast iron tawa on low heat. Cook on gentle dum for 30 minutes.

    ✓ Visual Cue: Steam is pressurized inside the pot, cooking the chicken in its own juices
  5. 05

    Rest & Fluff with Flat Ladle

    ⏱️ 15m

    Turn off heat and let rest unopened for 15 minutes. Cut open the dough seal. Dig a flat plate or ladle vertically down to lift all layers together without breaking long rice grains.

    ✓ Visual Cue: Incomparable burst of saffron, mint, and tender juicy chicken aroma
💡

Chef's Authentic Tips

  • Salting the boiling rice water until it tastes like seawater ensures the rice itself is flavorful.
  • Cooking over a heavy tawa on low heat prevents the bottom layer of chicken from burning while creating a rich masala crust (tahdig).
🔄

Smart Substitutions

  • For a vegetarian version, substitute chicken with mixed veggies, paneer, and soya chunks marinated similarly.
🍽️

Serving Suggestions & Pairings

  • ✓ Serve with Mirchi Ka Salan (spicy chili-peanut gravy) and chilled onion-cucumber raita.
