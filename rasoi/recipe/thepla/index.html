Gujarati Methi Thepla

4.9 (1,240 ratings)
• Cuisine: Gujarati

Thin, spiced, soft whole wheat flatbreads kneaded with fresh fenugreek leaves, yogurt, sesame seeds, and turmeric. The ultimate travel food with remarkable shelf life.

⏱️ Prep Time 15 min
🍳 Cook Time 15 min
⌛ Total Time 30 min
🍽️ Base Servings 4
👨‍🍳 Difficulty Easy
🌶️ Spice Level Mild
🥕

Ingredients

Servings:
4

For the Thepla Dough

  • 250 g whole wheat flour (gehun ka atta)— fine
  • 2 tbsp besan (gram flour)— adds softness and nutty taste
  • 100 g fresh fenugreek leaves (methi)— washed, drained thoroughly, and very finely chopped
  • 3 tbsp fresh curd (yogurt)— makes dough soft for days
  • 1 tbsp white sesame seeds (til)— subtle crunch
  • 1/2 tsp carom seeds (ajwain)— crushed
  • 1 tbsp ginger-green chilli paste— fine
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric powder— golden
  • 1 tsp coriander-cumin powder (dhana jeera)— ground
  • 1/2 tsp Kashmiri red chilli powder— mild warmth
  • 2 tbsp cooking oil— kneaded into dough plus extra for roasting
  • 1 tsp salt— to taste
🔥

Cooking Instructions

  1. 01

    Knead Spiced Methi Dough

    ⏱️ 8m

    In a wide bowl, combine wheat flour, besan, chopped methi leaves, sesame seeds, ajwain, ginger-chilli paste, turmeric, dhana jeera, chilli powder, salt, yogurt, and 2 tbsp oil. Knead into a soft, smooth dough using minimal water. Rest for 10 minutes.

    ✓ Visual Cue: A soft, pliable green-flecked golden dough
  2. 02

    Roll into Thin Disks

    ⏱️ 6m

    Divide dough into small lemon-sized balls. Dust lightly with dry flour and roll each into a very thin, uniform 7-inch circle.

    ✓ Visual Cue: Thin, even disks with embedded methi leaves and sesame seeds
  3. 03

    Roast on Hot Tawa

    ⏱️ 2m

    Heat a tawa on medium-high heat. Place rolled thepla on tawa. Cook for 30 seconds until tiny bubbles appear. Flip over.

    ✓ Visual Cue: Gentle bubbles on the surface
  4. 04

    Press with Oil to Keep Soft

    ⏱️ 2m

    Drizzle 1/2 tsp oil around edges and on top. Press gently with a flat spatula, cooking for 30-45 seconds per side until light golden specks appear. Do not overcook or thepla will become brittle.

    ✓ Visual Cue: Soft and pliable with light golden brown dots
  5. 05

    Stack & Store

    ⏱️ 1m

    Stack cooked theplas on top of each other immediately so steam keeps them pillowy soft. Serve warm or pack for travel.

    ✓ Visual Cue: Aromatic fenugreek and sesame fragrance
💡

Chef's Authentic Tips

  • Chop the methi leaves very finely and dry thoroughly before kneading; wet leaves make the dough sticky.
  • Cook thepla quickly on medium-high flame with oil; slow roasting dries them out into hard papads.
🔄

Smart Substitutions

  • Use kasuri methi (crushed dry fenugreek) if fresh leaves are out of season (use 3 tbsp soaked in 2 tbsp water).
🍽️

Serving Suggestions & Pairings

  • ✓ Classic pairing with sweet mango pickle (chundo), spiced curd, or a cup of hot masala chai.
