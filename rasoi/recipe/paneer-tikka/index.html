Paneer Tikka

4.9 (1,120 ratings)
• Cuisine: North Indian / Tandoori

Marinated firm cottage cheese cubes, bell peppers, and red onions skewered and roasted until charred and blistered, sprinkled with tangy chaat masala.

⏱️ Prep Time 25 min
🍳 Cook Time 15 min
⌛ Total Time 40 min
🍽️ Base Servings 4
👨‍🍳 Difficulty Easy
🌶️ Spice Level Medium
🥕

Ingredients

Servings:
4

For the Paneer and Veggies

  • 400 g firm paneer— cut into thick 1-inch squares
  • 1 large green bell pepper— cut into 1-inch squares
  • 1 large red bell pepper— cut into 1-inch squares
  • 1 large red onion— layers separated into petals

For the Tandoori Tikka Marinade

  • 150 g hung curd (Greek yogurt)— thick and strained
  • 2 tbsp roasted gram flour (besan)— lightly toasted in a dry pan until aromatic
  • 2 tbsp mustard oil— heated until smoking, then cooled slightly
  • 1 1/2 tbsp ginger-garlic paste— freshly made
  • 1 1/2 tbsp Kashmiri red chilli powder— vibrant natural red
  • 1/2 tsp ajwain (carom seeds)— crushed between palms
  • 1 tsp garam masala— warm ground spice
  • 1 tsp chaat masala— tangy finish
  • 1 tsp kasuri methi— crushed dried fenugreek
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice— fresh
  • 1/2 tsp black salt (kala namak)— imparts authentic tandoor punch
  • 0.8 tsp salt— to taste
🔥

Cooking Instructions

  1. 01

    Roast Besan & Smoke Mustard Oil

    ⏱️ 4m

    Dry roast besan in a small pan on low heat for 2 minutes until nutty and light fawn. Heat mustard oil until it reaches smoking point, remove from heat and let cool 1 minute. Add Kashmiri chilli powder directly into the warm mustard oil to unleash brilliant ruby red color.

    ✓ Visual Cue: A rich nutty roasted besan aroma and vibrant red oil base
  2. 02

    Prepare Tikka Marinade

    ⏱️ 5m

    In a wide mixing bowl, combine hung curd, the infused warm red mustard oil, roasted besan, ginger-garlic paste, ajwain, garam masala, chaat masala, kasuri methi, lemon juice, black salt, and regular salt. Whisk until silky and lump-free.

    ✓ Visual Cue: Thick clinging paste that coats the back of a spoon
  3. 03

    Coat Paneer & Skewer

    ⏱️ 20m

    Gently add paneer cubes, bell peppers, and onion petals to the marinade. Coat each piece carefully using clean hands to avoid breaking the paneer. Marinate for 20-30 minutes. Thread alternatively (onion, pepper, paneer, pepper) onto soaked wooden or metal skewers.

    ✓ Visual Cue: Pieces should have a generous blanket of marinade
  4. 04

    Grill or Bake to Blistering

    ⏱️ 12m

    Preheat oven to 220°C (430°F) or heat a stovetop cast iron grill pan with 1 tbsp oil. Cook skewers for 10-12 minutes, turning halfway and brushing with melted butter, until edges are blistered and lightly charred.

    ✓ Visual Cue: Smoky char marks appear on paneer corners while core remains juicy
  5. 05

    Dust & Serve

    ⏱️ 2m

    Transfer skewers to a platter, dust generously with tangy chaat masala, and drizzle fresh lemon juice.

    ✓ Visual Cue: Mouthwatering sizzle and spicy tangy fragrance
💡

Chef's Authentic Tips

  • Roasted besan acts as a binder, preventing the yogurt marinade from dripping off into the pan during grilling.
  • Smoked mustard oil combined with ajwain delivers that unmistakable authentic Delhi street dhaba flavor.
🔄

Smart Substitutions

  • Firm extra pressed tofu can be substituted for a 100% vegan tikka.
  • Can also add button mushrooms and baby corn to the skewers.
🍽️

Serving Suggestions & Pairings

  • ✓ Serve hot with spicy mint-coriander chutney, thinly sliced onion rings (laccha pyaz), and lemon wedges.
