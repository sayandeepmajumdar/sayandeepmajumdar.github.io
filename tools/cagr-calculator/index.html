💡 What is CAGR and Why Does It Matter?

Annualized Compounding

Unlike simple absolute return, CAGR smooths out annual volatility to show the steady rate at which an investment would have grown if it compounded evenly.

Comparing Apples to Apples

CAGR allows you to fairly compare a 3-year mutual fund return against a 7-year real estate investment or a 10-year index fund.

Formula Transparency

CAGR = (EV / BV)^(1/n) - 1 where EV is Ending Value, BV is Beginning Value, and n is number of years.
