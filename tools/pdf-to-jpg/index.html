💡 How In-Browser PDF Conversion Works

1. 100% Client-Side Privacy

Mozilla's PDF.js WebAssembly engine compiles and executes entirely inside your browser. No files, text, or images are ever transmitted across the internet.

2. Crisp Vector Rasterization

Unlike bitmap screenshots, PDF vector paths and fonts are dynamically re-rasterized at chosen DPI scales (up to 300 DPI) for razor-sharp typography and diagrams.

3. Instant Batch ZIP Export

Converted images are packaged on-the-fly into a clean `.zip` archive via JSZip, allowing you to download 50+ converted pages with a single click.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is it really safe to convert confidential or legal PDFs here? ▼

Yes, absolutely. Unlike cloud-based converters that upload your files to external remote servers, Toolzy's converter runs 100% locally in your browser's JavaScript sandbox. You can even disconnect your internet connection after opening this page and it will continue to work offline!

Why does my converted JPG have a white background instead of transparent? ▼

The JPEG specification does not support alpha transparency. Without a solid background, transparent PDF areas would turn pitch black. If you need transparent backgrounds, select PNG format and enable the "PNG Transparency" toggle.

Can I convert password-protected PDFs? ▼

Yes. If your PDF is encrypted, our tool will automatically open an inline password dialog. The decryption happens strictly in your browser using standard PDF crypto algorithms.
